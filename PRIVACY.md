# Privacy Policy for X Blur Mode

**Last Updated: August 2026**

**X Blur Mode** is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
X Blur Mode does **not** collect, store, transmit, log, track, or share any personal information, browsing history, message contents, account credentials, or user data of any kind.

### 2. Local Processing Only
All operations performed by the Extension (such as blurring handles, avatars, display names, media, and direct messages) execute entirely within your browser client on the local device. No webpage content or text is ever captured or sent to external servers.

### 3. Storage and Permissions
- **`storage` Permission**: The Extension uses browser storage (`chrome.storage.sync` and `chrome.storage.local`) exclusively to save your chosen toggle preferences (e.g., whether to blur handles or reveal on hover) across your browser sessions. This data remains on your device or in your personal browser sync profile.
- **No Remote Code**: The Extension contains no remote scripts, telemetry, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics libraries.

### 4. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not integrate with, communicate with, or transfer data to any third-party services or APIs.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
If any changes are made to this Privacy Policy in future updates, they will be posted on this page.

### 6. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official web store listing support page.
